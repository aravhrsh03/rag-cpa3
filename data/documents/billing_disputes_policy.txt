PRODAPT
Internal Policy Document

Document ID: POL-BILL-003
Title: Billing Disputes, Duplicate Charges, and Credit Approval
Version: 5.0
Effective date: 2026-02-01
Owner: Billing Resolution
Classification: Internal. Required for any credit posted to a Prodapt account.
Applies to: All consumer, business, and enterprise bills.

1. Purpose

This policy controls how long a customer has to dispute a charge, which charges may be credited, and when a credit may be applied immediately versus when it must wait for a supervisor.

Account balances, charge lines, and duplicate flags are not stored in this document. They are in the billing database. An agent must look up the account before quoting a balance or stating that a duplicate exists.

2. Dispute window

A customer has 60 days from the charge date to open a dispute.
The charge date is the date on the charge line, not the date the customer noticed it.
A dispute opened on day 60 is still in time. A dispute opened on day 61 is late and must be declined unless a billing supervisor grants a one-time exception, which is recorded on the dispute.
The window applies to plan charges, add-ons, roaming, device installments, taxes billed in error, and late fees.

How to answer "how long do I have to dispute a charge?":
You have 60 days from the charge date.

3. What may be credited

- A duplicate charge: the same description and the same billing period posted more than once, or a charge line with the duplicate flag set.
- A roaming charge that violates the roaming policy (for example Travel Pass plus pay-per-use for the same data). Ordinary published roaming rates are not credits.
- An SLA credit that the SLA policy says is owed.
- A device installment or add-on the customer did not authorize, when the order system has no matching order.
- A tax billed on a tax-exempt enterprise account, with the exemption certificate already on file.

4. What must not be credited

- Usage the customer actually incurred at a published rate.
- A charge older than 60 days, unless a supervisor exception is on the dispute.
- A second credit for the same charge id.
- Cash, check, or external refund. Credits reduce the Prodapt account balance or sit pending. They are not sent as a payment to the customer.
- An amount that is still only a guess. Look up the charge before choosing the credit amount.

5. How to identify a duplicate

Treat a charge as a duplicate when either of these is true:

a. The charge line's duplicate flag is set, or
b. Another charge for the same customer has the same description and the same billing period.

The credit amount for a duplicate is the amount of the extra line, once. If two identical plan charges posted, credit one of them, not both. Leave one valid charge on the account.
If the flag is set and the description also matches another line in that period, that is one duplicate, not two.

6. Auto-approval limit

The auto-approval limit is 50.00 USD per credit.

- If the credit amount is 50.00 USD or less, the credit is auto-approved.
  Record the credit with status APPLIED.
  Reduce the account's current balance by the credit amount.
  The new balance must not go below zero. If it would, apply only enough to bring the balance to zero and leave a note. Do not create a cash refund for the remainder.

- If the credit amount is greater than 50.00 USD, the credit is not auto-approved.
  Record the credit with status PENDING_APPROVAL.
  Do not change the current balance.
  The balance changes only after a billing supervisor approves the row and the status becomes APPLIED.
  Tell the customer the credit is submitted and waiting for approval. Do not say the credit has already been applied.

A supervisor may reject a pending credit. Set the status to REJECTED and leave the balance unchanged.

Credits above 200.00 USD follow the same PENDING_APPROVAL rule and also require a manager, not only a billing supervisor. The agent still must not reduce the balance at the time of the request.

Worked examples:

Example A. A duplicate monthly plan charge of 65.99 USD.
65.99 is greater than 50.00. Insert the credit as PENDING_APPROVAL. Do not reduce the balance. One valid plan charge remains.

Example B. A duplicate add-on of 12.00 USD.
12.00 is less than or equal to 50.00. Insert the credit as APPLIED and reduce the current balance by 12.00.

Example C. A credit of exactly 50.00 USD.
50.00 does not exceed the limit. Status APPLIED. Reduce the balance by 50.00.

7. One dispute per charge

Open at most one active dispute (status OPEN or ESCALATED) per charge id.
If a dispute is already OPEN, do not open a second one and do not post a second credit. Update the existing dispute.
When a credit moves to APPLIED, set the related dispute to RESOLVED and record the credit id in the resolution notes.
When a credit is REJECTED, set the dispute to RESOLVED and state why it was denied.

8. Escalation

Escalate to a billing supervisor, and set the dispute status to ESCALATED, when:

- The credit is greater than 50.00 USD.
- The customer asks for more than the duplicate amount.
- The dispute is outside the 60 day window and the customer is asking for an exception.
- The account is Enterprise.
- Fraud is suspected (the customer denies the line entirely, or charges appear from a country the customer could not have visited). Fraud cases go to the fraud desk, not to a standard credit.

9. Audit

Every credit must be a row in the billing credits table with a customer id, an amount, a reason, a status, and a timestamp.
Do not change a balance with a manual note only. If the balance changes, an APPLIED credit row must explain the change.
Do not keep a side list of credits outside the billing database.
The reason text should name the charge description and the billing period, for example "Duplicate Unlimited Plus charge for 2026-09".

10. What support should tell the customer

State whether a duplicate was found, the amount, and whether the credit is APPLIED or PENDING_APPROVAL.
If the status is PENDING_APPROVAL, give the customer the dispute id and say a supervisor must approve credits over 50.00 USD.
If no duplicate exists, say so, and do not create a credit to be helpful.
Quote document id POL-BILL-003 when asked which policy was used.

11. SLA credits posted by billing

When the SLA policy says a customer is eligible, billing posts that dollar amount under this policy.
The SLA policy chooses eligibility and the formula. This policy chooses APPLIED versus PENDING_APPROVAL.
A 22.50 USD Business SLA credit is APPLIED.
A 62.25 USD Enterprise SLA credit is PENDING_APPROVAL because it is over 50.00 USD.

12. Same description is not always a duplicate

A plan that bills every month will show the same description in 2026-07, 2026-08, and 2026-09. Those are three periods, not a duplicate.
A duplicate requires the same customer, the same description, and the same billing period, or a line whose duplicate flag is set.
Credit one extra line, not every line that shares the description.
If a duplicate was already credited and that credit is APPLIED, do not credit it again. A resolved dispute is the record.
A PAID invoice from a prior month is history. Do not subtract that history from the current balance a second time.

A duplicate Unlimited Plus charge of 65.99 USD is the over-limit case. Insert PENDING_APPROVAL. Leave the current balance unchanged. One valid 65.99 USD plan charge stays on the account.
A duplicate International Day Pass of 12.00 USD is the under-limit case. Insert APPLIED and reduce the balance by 12.00.
A duplicate Japan pay-per-use line of 36.00 USD is under the limit. APPLIED, reduce by 36.00.
A duplicate device installment of 80.00 USD is over the limit. PENDING_APPROVAL, balance unchanged.
A duplicate Travel Pass bundle of exactly 50.00 USD does not exceed the limit. APPLIED, reduce by 50.00.

14. Charges that look wrong but are not duplicates

A single Travel Pass or pay-per-use line at a price published in the roaming policy is not a duplicate. Examples: 60.00 USD for five Japan Zone C days (5 times 12.00), or a single Brazil Zone D data line. Decline the dispute. Do not insert a credit.
A sales-tax line next to a plan charge is not a duplicate of the plan. Different description.
A late fee is not a duplicate of the plan. If the payment posted after the due date, the late fee stands and any requested credit is REJECTED.
Zone A pay-per-use data on an Unlimited Plus line is a real billing error, because Unlimited Plus includes Canada and Mexico. Credit that pay-per-use amount under section 6. If that credit is already APPLIED, stop.
5G Home cannot roam. A roaming line on a 5G Home account is a billing error. Credit it if it is still inside 60 days and has not already been credited.

15. Lookup rules

Read the account from the billing database before quoting a balance, a name, or a charge.
The current balance equals the open charges, minus credits that were APPLIED against those open charges. Paid charges from prior months are not added again.
Return the open charge lines, including which ones have the duplicate flag, the description, the amount, and the billing period.
If a dispute is already OPEN or ESCALATED for that charge, do not open another dispute and do not post a second credit.
A CLOSED account with a zero balance has nothing to credit.
A SUSPENDED account can still dispute an open charge. Suspension does not waive the 60-day window and does not by itself create a credit. A suspended account with a past-due balance over 25.00 USD also cannot start a device upgrade. That rule is in the device upgrade policy. It does not change the credit limit here.

16. What the customer should hear

Duplicate found, amount 50.00 USD or less: say the credit has been applied and give the new balance.
Duplicate found, amount over 50.00 USD: say the credit is waiting for supervisor approval and the balance is unchanged until then.
No duplicate, published rate: say the charge matches the rate card and no credit was posted.
Already credited: say the earlier credit stands and quote the resolved dispute.
Outside 60 days: say the window has closed. Day 60 is still in time. Day 61 is late.

17. Document control

The dispute window is 60 days. The auto-approval limit is 50.00 USD. Amounts greater than 50.00 USD use status PENDING_APPROVAL and do not change the balance until approval. Version 5.0 did not raise the limit.
